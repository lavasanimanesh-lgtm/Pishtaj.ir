Who We Are

Pishro Tajhiz Fartak (PTF) is a specialized industrial equipment supplier based in Tehran, Iran. Since our founding in 2014 (registered under No. 578831), we have been serving the Oil & Gas, Petrochemical, Steel, Cement, and Power Generation industries with high-quality piping, valves, instrumentation, and electrical equipment.

Our Expertise

We supply materials and equipment for both upstream and downstream sectors. Our team of experienced engineers understands the technical requirements of EPC projects, including compliance with international standards (ASME, API, ASTM, ISO, IEC) and project-specific Vendor Lists (NIOC, NIGC, NPC).

Our Commitment

Every supply we deliver comes with full technical documentation including Mill Test Certificates (MTC 3.1), material traceability, and third-party inspection reports when requested. We are committed to providing the highest quality products at competitive prices with the shortest possible delivery times.

Company Profile

Pishro Tajhiz Fartak supplies industrial equipment to energy and process-plant projects, working with EPC contractors, plant operators and maintenance teams. Our scope spans piping materials, valves, instrumentation, electrical equipment and rotating machinery — always tied to project documentation: datasheets, vendor lists, inspection and test plans, and delivery schedules.

What differentiates us is engineering-led procurement: a technical review happens before pricing, so offers reflect what your project actually accepts. From routine MRO supply to shutdown spares and capital packages, we coordinate sourcing, QA and logistics end to end. See our services and project references.

Frequently asked questions

Which industries do you serve?

Oil and gas, petrochemicals, refineries, steel plants and power stations — mainly EPC contractors and plant operators.

How do you verify technical compliance?

Each RFQ is matched against the client vendor list, datasheets and applicable standards (API, ASME, ASTM, IEC) before quotation.

Can you support urgent shutdown requirements?

Yes. We handle emergency spares with prioritized sourcing and expedited delivery options.
