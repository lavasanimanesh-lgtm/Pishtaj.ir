Industrial Equipment Supply & Procurement Services

Comprehensive industrial equipment supply and procurement services for Oil & Gas, Petrochemical, Steel and Power Generation projects.

Piping & Flanges

Seamless pipe A106 Gr.B, A333 Gr.6, API 5L Gr.B/X42-X70. Flanges WN/SO/BL ASME B16.5. Butt-weld fittings, gaskets, bolting. All with MTC 3.1 certification.

Industrial Valves

Gate valve API 600, Globe valve, Ball valve API 6D (Floating & Trunnion), Check valve, Butterfly valve API 609. Control valves, PSV, actuators, positioners.

Electrical Equipment

LV switchgear, ACB (Siemens 3WL, ABB Emax 2, Schneider MTZ), MCCB, VCB, PLC (Siemens S7-1500, Allen Bradley), VFD, UPS, transformers.

Instrumentation

Pressure transmitters (Rosemount 3051, Yokogawa EJA), flowmeters (Magmeter, Coriolis, Vortex), radar level transmitters, temperature sensors, control valves.

Inspection & Quality

Third Party Inspection (TPI), NDT testing (UT, RT, MT, PT), MTC 3.1/3.2, PMI testing, NACE MR0175 certification, calibration certificates.

Procurement & Logistics

International procurement from Europe and Asia. Customs clearance, shipping (FOB/CIF/DDP). Complete supply chain management for EPC projects.

How We Work

Every engagement starts with your RFQ package: datasheets, quantities, vendor list and delivery terms. Our engineers verify compliance line by line, source from approved manufacturers, and return a firm offer with documentation commitments. After order placement we expedite production, coordinate inspection and testing, and manage packing, export and delivery to site.

Services can be engaged separately or as a managed package: supply of piping, valves, instrumentation and electrical equipment; inspection and quality coordination; procurement and logistics for multi-vendor consolidated shipments. Read our company profile or review delivered case studies.

Our teams work against the standards that govern this scope — ASME B31.3/B16.5, API 600/6D/610, NACE MR0175 and IEC — and with the documentation habits of EPC owners, so your RFQ is answered against the correct reference rather than a generic catalogue quote.

Frequently asked questions

What does your procurement service include?

Sourcing from approved manufacturers, bid evaluation, expediting, QA documentation and delivery coordination — as a package or per line item.

Do you coordinate third-party inspection?

Yes. TPI attendance, NDT witnessing, PMI and calibration-certificate verification can be arranged and documented.

Can you consolidate multi-vendor packages?

Yes. We consolidate equipment from several manufacturers into scheduled deliveries with unified tagging and documentation.
