Why PTF for Energy Projects

Pishro Tajhiz Fartak (PTF) is an industrial equipment supplier focused on oil, gas, petrochemical, steel and power projects. We cover piping, valves, instrumentation, electrical equipment and pumps — from a single emergency spare to full project material packages. Every offer is checked against your approved vendor list before pricing, so technical compliance is confirmed up front rather than discovered at delivery.

Quality assurance is built into the process: mill test certificates (MTC 3.1/3.2), third-party inspection, NDT and PMI testing can be arranged per contract. Our engineers support datasheet review, expediting and delivery coordination to site. Learn more about us, review our services, or see project case studies.

Frequently asked questions

Which equipment categories do you supply?

Piping (pipes, flanges, fittings), industrial valves, instrumentation, electrical equipment, and pumps — sourced against project vendor lists with full QA documentation.

How do I request a quotation?

Send your RFQ with datasheets, quantities and delivery terms; our engineers review it against approved vendor lists and return a priced offer with documentation.

Do you provide mill certificates and inspection?

Yes. MTC 3.1/3.2, third-party inspection (TPI), NDT and PMI can be arranged per contract requirements.
