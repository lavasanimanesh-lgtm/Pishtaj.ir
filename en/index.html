FAQ

Questions buyers ask first

Which equipment categories do you supply?

Piping (pipe, flanges, fittings), industrial valves, instrumentation, electrical equipment, pumps and compressors — against project vendor lists with full QA documents.

How do I request a quotation?

Send datasheets, quantities, Incoterms and the approved vendor list. We review each line and return a priced offer with documentation commitments.

Do you provide mill certificates and inspection?

Yes. MTC 3.1/3.2, TPI (Bureau Veritas, SGS, DNV), NDT and PMI can be arranged per contract.

Can you match a project-specific vendor list?

Vendor-list compliance is a core skill. We work the brands used on NIOC, NIGC, NPC and major EPC lists, and support VAR when needed.
